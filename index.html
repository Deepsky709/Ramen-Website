All History Comes From a Bowl

Welcome to the Miso Ramen Museum!

Here, you can dig as much knowledge as possible about the origin of this iconic dish as well as learn the art of creating it yourself. While there are many different kinds of ramen like shoyu, shio, miso, and tonkotsu, we believe that Miso ramen stands apart the most. It is not just a regular bowl of noodles, but a combination of rich soup and crafted noodles with a fascinating history behind its flavor. Looking back at its journey, it evolved from a mere Chinese noodle to a special dish in Japan. This iconic dish carries a mind-blowing culture and meaning. What began as an essential food during crisis has now become one of Japan's most popular comfort dishes.
